Thanks for reporting this, and sorry, that one was on us. Cinematic puts an invisible strip along the top of windows (your bags included) so you can drag them by the title bar. The gamepad cursor treated that strip as something to stop on, which is the invisible thing at the top left of your bags.

The next update turns the strip off while gamepad mode is on, so the d-pad goes straight to your bag slots. You can still drag windows by their edges.

Until then you can get rid of it by typing this in chat:

/cine find Drag windows to move them

and untick "Drag windows to move them" (you lose dragging windows for now, but the bags behave).
