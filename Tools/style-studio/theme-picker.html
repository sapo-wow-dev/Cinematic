Theme picker

Three ways to pick a nameplate theme, each in Cinematic's panel at its in-game size. Every one shows all 19 choices as little plates in their own colors, not blank swatches. Hover a theme to see it on the big plate; click to pick it.

Which I'd pick

  • A if the page has room: everything visible, one click, nothing hidden behind "More…". It's the tallest.
  • B if space matters most: one line when closed, and the open list carries each theme's one-line description, so it's the easiest to choose from with 19 themes.
  • C splits "Follow main theme" out as a switch, which is what it really is. With it on, the strip shows which theme it's following and rests until you turn it off.

The plates are drawn from each theme's real colors (panel, edge, accent) and bar shape. In game they'd use the real bar textures.
