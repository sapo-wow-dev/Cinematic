What changed from now

1

Solid page. The game no longer shows through Cinematic's side, so text sits on one even color. The game's list on the left keeps its look (it's the game's).

2

One tab row. Default, Layout, Smart and Cast Bars sit as plain tabs under the icons, each with its on/off dot (right-click toggles it, as now). Names has one page, so no tab row.

3

Gold only for what's picked. Buttons are text, not gold boxes. Undo and Redo are words in the footer, shown only when there's a step to take. No Copy look or Reset for now.

4

Preview beside the settings, not above them. At this window size there's room for both, so the first setting is at the top, not at the bottom edge.

5

Fewer dropdowns. What the preview shows is a row of toggles under it; parts are tabs, or click the part on the plate.

6

Smart rows open in place. Click a row and its look opens under it with the same editor as Default. Drag rows to reorder.

7

Layout is dragging. Drag the parts on the plate; the numbers follow. "Layout for" picks which plates get their own layout.

8

No greyed dead rows. "Plate color: Next row's" becomes a sentence saying where the color comes from, with a link to pick your own.

9

Theme is one line that opens a list. Each theme shows as a small plate in its own colors, with a line saying what it is. Find box, preview on hover, closes on pick, click outside or Esc.
